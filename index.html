Making Quotes More Comparable

The way to make quotes comparable is to ask every shortlisted contractor the same practical questions, then note where answers are vague. You do not need technical jargon to do this well. You need a consistent brief. Describe the site condition, the rough wall length, and whether access is tight or straightforward. Ask what is included in the quoted scope, and what would trigger a variation. Ask whether the contractor wants photos first, a site visit first, or both. Ask who will be your point of contact once the work starts. If a business cannot explain its process clearly at quote stage, that is useful information.
